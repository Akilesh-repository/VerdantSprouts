The Journey

Seed to Plate Process.

We follow a strict, sustainable procedure to guarantee maximum flavor and nutrition in every batch.

Phase 01

We carefully select non-GMO, organic seeds and plant them in a nutrient-rich, soil-free coconut coir medium.

Phase 02

Harvested precisely at the first true leaf stage to ensure the absolute highest concentration of vitamins.
