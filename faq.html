Accounts & Logistics

Subscriptions & Delivery

How do I pause or cancel my subscription?

You can easily pause, skip a week, or cancel your subscription at any time through our online client portal. Simply log in, navigate to 'Manage Subscription', and update your delivery calendar before the weekly cutoff time (Tuesdays at 5 PM).

Do you deliver outside of the main city limits?

Currently, we guarantee free delivery within a 25-mile radius of our downtown greenhouse. If you live outside this zone, we offer a specialized courier delivery for a small flat-rate shipping fee. Please check our Delivery Zones map for exact boundaries.

What happens if I am not home during delivery?

All our microgreens are delivered in thermal-insulated, compostable cooler bags with a reusable ice pack. They will remain perfectly fresh and crisp on your shaded porch for up to 6 hours after drop-off.

Can I customize which greens are in my weekly box?

Yes! While our 'Farmer's Choice' mix is the most popular, you can customize your box through the portal. You can select specific varieties like pure sunflower shoots, radish, or broccoli depending on your dietary preferences.

Science & Sourcing

Growing Methods & Quality

Are your microgreens certified organic?

We use 100% non-GMO, organic seeds and never apply synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or fertilizers. Because we grow hydroponically indoors, traditional soil-based organic certifications don't perfectly apply, but our practices exceed standard organic requirements.

Should I wash the microgreens before eating them?

Our commercial packs are triple-washed in a sterilized facility. However, for residential deliveries, we recommend a gentle, quick rinse under cold water right before consumption, just as you would with any fresh produce.

How long do the greens stay fresh in the fridge?

If kept in their original compostable clamshells in the crisper drawer of your refrigerator, our microgreens will stay perfectly crisp and vibrant for 10 to 14 days. Make sure not to let them freeze!

What is the growing medium if you don't use soil?

We utilize a sterilized, pH-balanced coconut coir mat. This allows the roots to weave securely without the mess or potential soil-borne pathogens of dirt, ensuring an impeccably clean harvest every time.
